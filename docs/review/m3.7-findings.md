## Accuracy review

Every shown model card was checked, title and each sentence, against the snippets it cites only (five parallel reviewers, strict rules: added facts, joined facts, changed attribution, dropped hedges, misleading in context). 52 of 109 cards have at least one problem; about 8 of those are minor or borderline.

What goes wrong, roughly by frequency:

1. **Joined or reassigned facts.** Teva "approved" an injector (it gained approval to market one); Hőgyes "confirmed" a finding the source credits to two people; cooking fuel listed as a natural carbon monoxide source; dynamite said to contain nitroglycerin when the cited snippet doesn't say so.
2. **Headline titles that invent.** "Ripening fruit and plastic bags", "From your kitchen to lab tables", "In birth, in love, in food", "From skin color to sleep aid", "Ibuprofen cuts Parkinson's risk by 38%".
3. **Dropped hedges.** "may have been named" → named; "is considered to have" → did; "anecdotal account" → fact; "in some people" → everyone; "was estimated at" → was.
4. **Misleading in context.** Quinine for leg cramps "not recommended due to serious side effects" → "side effects are a concern"; "used in the past as a solvent" → used as a solvent.

The claim check (the same 4B model, verdict style) passed all of these. It catches crude inventions, but not subtle ones, and it still flags faithful paraphrases, which is why trimming helped the card count.

## A deterministic check?

Most failures add a word the sources don't have. Counting card words (not stop words, crude stemming) missing from the cited snippets catches 51 of the 52 bad cards, but also 54 of the 57 good ones: ordinary paraphrase brings "found", "appears", "like". It can't serve as a gate on freely written text, and it misses reassigned facts entirely.

## Conclusion

Free rewriting by a 4B model, checked by itself, doesn't get near zero invented claims. Reaching the target means constraining what the model may write, not checking harder afterwards. Options are in the M3.7 entry of `docs/PLAN.md` once decided.
